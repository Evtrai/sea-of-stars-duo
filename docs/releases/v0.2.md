Thor Edition: the bottom screen is redrawn in the game's own style and does much more.

**Battle**
- Battle board with enemy cards: turn countdown, spell locks (shown closed until the game reveals them) and the moves that break them.
- The game's info bar and instruction banner, the command, skill and item lists (tap to pick), combo points, the ultimate gauge and Live Mana.
- Party panels with HP/MP and the KO countdown.
- A small TOP HUD switch makes the top-screen HUD transparent for everything the bottom screen already shows.

**Exploration**
- A chart of the area you are in, drawn from the level's walkable ground, with the party marker and the current objective.
- On the overworld, the island chart with the party marker; a button opens the game's own world map.

**Menus on the bottom screen**
- Equip, Skills (skills, combos, ultimate), Items (use food), Order (swap party members) and Relics (toggle), available whenever the game would let you open its menu.
- Every change goes through the game's own functions, the same ones its menu calls.

**Fixes**
- Button prompts on the top screen for pads the game has no pictures for (AYN Thor): lettered A/B/X/Y instead of a "missing button" picture.
- A back button could stay on the top screen after its list closed.

**Known limitations**
- Developed and tested on the AYN Thor only, against Sea of Stars 3.0.60158.
- Stand-alone button icons (for example the Boost trigger) still use the game's generic pad pictures.
- Saves are separate from the Play game's; copy them over yourself (see README).
- Builds are signed with a throwaway key unless the repository has signing secrets, so an update may need an uninstall first (back up your saves).
