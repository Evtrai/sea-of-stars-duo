First packaged build.

- Boots your Google Play install of Sea of Stars on the top screen, unmodified.
- Bottom-screen companion with Battle, Party and Items tabs, live data and localized names.
- Battle tab: enemy turn countdowns, spell locks and HP, combo points, Live Mana.
- Analytics/crash reporting of the hosted game is switched off.

Known limitations
- Touch battle commands (Attack / Skills) don't advance the game yet; use the controller.
- Play time can show 0:00/0:01.
- Saves are separate from the Play game's; copy them over yourself (see README).
